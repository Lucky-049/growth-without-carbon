Growth Without Carbon? Which Big Economies Broke the Link

The question. Among the 20 largest CO₂ emitters, which ones grew their economy between 2005 and 2022 while cutting emissions, and how fast is the gap between rich and emerging economies closing?

Method. GDP is inflation adjusted and measured in international dollars (Maddison Project, latest year 2022), so the 2005 to 2022 window is the longest one with both series. CO₂ covers fossil fuels and industry, excluding land use. Carbon intensity equals CO₂ divided by GDP. Country list is the 20 largest emitters in 2022.
